Ask the owner first, and record it with `bin/jarvis approval request`, before:
spending money, launching campaigns, changing ad budgets, editing live Shopify pages, changing prices, contacting suppliers, deleting important business data, or any other irreversible action.

- Shopify work starts as drafts and previews. Never change the live storefront without approval.
- Meta work is read-only analytics plus draft preparation until the owner approves an action.
- Extra AI operating costs target about **A$50/month** (excluding Meta spend, Shopify fees and existing subscriptions). Before proposing a paid tool, state why it's needed, its monthly cost in AUD, usage limits, separate API charges, free alternatives, and whether it can wait. Do not add a new paid subscription without approval.
- Use the minimum permissions needed. Store credentials outside this repository (it is public).
