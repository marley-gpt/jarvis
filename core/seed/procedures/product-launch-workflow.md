When the owner says "test this product", run these steps (parallel where independent):

1. Evaluate product → `product-research`
2. Analyse customer demand, pain points, objections, angles → `market-research`
3. Find competitor advertising and inspiration → `creative-research`
   (steps 1–3 can run in parallel once the product is registered)
4. Develop the three strongest angles, concepts, image briefs and copy → `creative-director`
5. Ad images: owner generates them in ChatGPT from the briefs and imports them to `brain/files/<product>/ads/`
6. Prepare the Shopify product page draft → `shopify`
7. Prepare the Meta campaign draft → `meta-ads`
8. Check every deliverable against the mentor procedures → JARVIS (+ `mentor-learning` lookups)
9. Present everything for approval → JARVIS
10. Track results after launch → `product-testing`
11. Record lessons and next actions → JARVIS (`decide`, `mem add -c decisions`)
